# Product Requirements

## Product concept

**Working name:** CivicKey USB  
**Category:** Keypad-access USB flash drive  
**App context:** Fictional product for a Streamlit e-commerce learning demo

CivicKey USB is a portable storage product concept with a physical keypad.
Users enter a PIN on the device as an additional physical access-control step
before accessing stored files. The exact unlocking mechanism, encryption
implementation, capacity, compatibility, and durability are not defined by
this concept and must not be invented in product listings.

## Customer problem and value

People want portable storage that is easy to carry while adding a visible,
device-level step before files can be accessed. A physical keypad offers a
simple-to-understand product distinction for a demo store and creates an
opportunity to teach responsible handling of security claims.

The product should be described as adding an access-control step, not as
unhackable, encrypted, or guaranteed secure unless those claims are verified
for the actual device.

## Community-minded positioning

The store can tell a local-city-benefit story by presenting the product as a
community-minded small-business concept. Possible future examples include
support for digital literacy, local library technology, or e-waste collection.

No partnership, donation, percentage, or city endorsement is established by
this requirement. Until an owner configures verified terms, use wording such
as “Explore a future community contribution” and clearly label it as a
proposal. Do not imply that buying a demo product makes a donation.

## Intended audience

- People interested in portable storage and physical access controls.
- Local residents interested in thoughtfully designed, community-minded
  products.
- Students and developers learning e-commerce workflows.

## Product listing content

Each product record should support:

- Product name and short summary.
- Full description.
- Price and currency.
- Available inventory.
- Image or placeholder image.
- Search tags/category.
- Product status (published or unpublished).
- Optional, owner-verified specification fields, such as capacity or
  compatibility.
- Clear, accurate keypad/access-control description.

Only verified specification values should appear as product facts. A listing
must remain usable when optional specifications or imagery are missing.

## Demo catalog examples

The owner may seed fictional examples such as “CivicKey 32” and “CivicKey
Travel.” Any capacity in a sample name is illustrative demo data, not a claim
about a real product. Mark sample listings as demo products where that context
is visible.

## Pricing and availability

Owners control product price and stock. The catalog and product detail page
must reflect the current published status and available inventory. A product
with zero stock cannot be added to the cart for checkout.

## Trust and claim rules

- Identify this experience as a demo store.
- Do not claim that a keypad by itself encrypts files.
- Do not promise complete security, data recovery, or device compatibility.
- Do not claim a city partnership or donation program without verified terms.
- Do not present demo orders as paid or fulfilled purchases.
